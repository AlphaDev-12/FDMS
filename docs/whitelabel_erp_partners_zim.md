# FDMS Whitelabel ERP Partners in Zimbabwe (Grouped by ERP Ecosystem)

## Selection Criteria

All firms listed are **pure ERP consultancies and software integrators** that do **NOT** currently own or market a Virtual Fiscal Device (VFD) or FDMS API solution. They are prime candidates for a perpetual IP buyout of the FDMS platform.

### Excluded from Consideration:
* **Hardware Fiscal Device Sellers:** Axis Solutions, Cortech, Softrite, Microwarehouse, Global Horizons, Just I.T.
* **Existing VFD/FDMS Vendors:** CHIPS Business Solutions, Omni Africa, M&J Consultants, Relmax IT, Matiyas Solutions.
