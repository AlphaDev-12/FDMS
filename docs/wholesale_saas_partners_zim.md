# FDMS Wholesale SaaS Target Partners (Tier 3 & 4)

## Executive Summary

The following 10 IT and Accounting consultancies in Zimbabwe represent the ideal target market for the **Wholesale SaaS Model** ($60/device/year). 

These firms focus heavily on SME accounting software (QuickBooks, Sage Pastel, Xero, ERPNext). They typically lack the capital for a $10,000+ IP Buyout and the DevOps capabilities to host a resilient .NET application. However, they control hundreds of SME clients who urgently need ZIMRA fiscalisation. By offering them a hosted, white-labeled portal, we capture recurring revenue while empowering them to mark up the service to their clients.

---

## The Target 10 (SME / Cloud Accounting Focus)

### 1. Simply Accounting
* **Primary Ecosystem:** QuickBooks Desktop & QuickBooks Online
* **Profile:** Harare-based accounting advisory focused on practical QuickBooks setup and training for SMEs.
* **Why SaaS?** They are accountants, not software developers. A hosted portal they can slap their logo on is exactly what they need to monetize ZIMRA compliance for their client base.

### 2. Kudfort Zimbabwe
* **Primary Ecosystem:** Intuit QuickBooks (Desktop & Enterprise)
* **Profile:** Financial systems advisory serving corporate SMEs and retail operators. 
* **Why SaaS?** QuickBooks has zero native ZIMRA capability. Kudfort needs a plug-and-play middleware to prevent their clients from abandoning QuickBooks for a competitor.

### 3. Radical Systems
* **Primary Ecosystem:** Sage 50cloud Pastel Partner & Payroll
* **Profile:** Authorized Sage dealers in Zimbabwe focusing on SME bookkeeping, payroll, and VAT compliance.
* **Why SaaS?** Sage Pastel is the dominant on-premise SME software in Zimbabwe. Radical Systems can bulk-buy FDMS tokens and deploy them to their existing Pastel maintenance clients.

### 4. Step Business Solutions
* **Primary Ecosystem:** Sage Pastel Partner, Sage Xpress, Sage Business Cloud
* **Profile:** Located in Eastlea, Harare. They provide implementation and support for the lower end of the Sage product line.
* **Why SaaS?** Perfect candidate to resell a $150/year cloud fiscalisation bridge to small retailers and service businesses.

### 5. Zimbabwe Institute of Computerized Accounting (ZICA)
* **Primary Ecosystem:** Sage Pastel Evolution
* **Profile:** A training and implementation institute located in the Harare CBD. They install systems and train staff.
* **Why SaaS?** They can bundle the FDMS SaaS subscription as part of their "ZIMRA-ready certification" training packages for SMEs.

### 6. Infinity Edge
* **Primary Ecosystem:** Xero, Sage One, QuickBooks
* **Profile:** A modern, cloud-first consultancy run by accountants and IT specialists focusing on automation.
* **Why SaaS?** Cloud-native accountants want cloud-native tools. A webhook-based API SaaS fits perfectly into their Xero/Sage One automation workflows.

### 7. Surge Accounting
* **Primary Ecosystem:** Xero & Tax Advisory
* **Profile:** Provides company registration, tax administration, and cloud accounting implementation.
* **Why SaaS?** They handle ZIMRA/NSSA compliance for clients. A white-labeled FDMS portal allows them to offer "Fiscalisation-as-a-Service" alongside their tax consulting.

### 8. OyOsCloud (OyOs Systems)
* **Primary Ecosystem:** Cloud-hosted ERPNext, Zoho One
* **Profile:** A cloud ERP hosting platform serving multi-branch SMEs and local retailers.
* **Why SaaS?** They are already selling SaaS. Connecting their hosted ERPs to our hosted FDMS Gateway via API is a seamless architectural fit.

### 9. CyteERP Systems
* **Primary Ecosystem:** Sage Enterprise / SME Financials
* **Profile:** Implementation and software support firm for mid-market and SME clients.
* **Why SaaS?** Allows them to offer a managed ZIMRA compliance add-on without having to hire .NET backend developers.

### 10. Total IT Solutions
* **Primary Ecosystem:** Vimbika ERP (Proprietary Local ERP)
* **Profile:** Established local IT firm serving SMEs with their own proprietary management suite.
* **Why SaaS?** Instead of spending 6 months building ZIMRA cryptography into Vimbika, they can just proxy their API calls through our SaaS Gateway and instantly make Vimbika "ZIMRA Approved".
