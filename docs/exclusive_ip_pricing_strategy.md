## The Hybrid Alternative: Wholesale SaaS for Tier 3 & 4

Many smaller consultancies (e.g., QuickBooks or Sage One advisors) cannot afford a $10,000 upfront capital expenditure, nor do they want the technical burden of hosting a .NET 8 application with Redis and PostgreSQL databases. 

For these partners, we offer a **Wholesale SaaS Model** alongside the IP Buyout.

### How It Works
* **We Host It:** We run the FDMS Gateway on our cloud infrastructure as a multi-tenant SaaS.
* **White-Label Portal:** The partner uses the FDMS Branding API to upload their logo and custom CSS. Their clients log into a portal that looks like the partner's own software.
* **Pricing Model:** We charge the partner a wholesale rate of **$60/device/year**. 
* **Partner Margin:** The partner resells the service to their end-client for the market rate of **$150 – $200/device/year**. 
* **Billing:** The partner buys tokens in bulk (e.g., 50 device tokens for $3,000) and provisions them to their clients via the API.

### Why This is Highly Profitable

| Metric | Scenario |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Partner Incentive** | Zero upfront capital cost. Zero infrastructure maintenance. Guaranteed $90–$140 recurring profit per client per year. |
| **Our Revenue** | If we sign 10 lower-tier partners, and they average 30 clients each, that is **300 active devices**. |
| **Recurring Income** | 300 devices × $60 = **$18,000 USD per year** in pure recurring SaaS revenue. |

> **Strategic Note:** The codebase is *already built for this*. The `PartnerManagementService.cs`, `TenantRegistryService.cs`, and `WholesaleTokenReconciliationWorker.cs` modules were designed specifically to support multi-tenant token billing and white-labeling out of the box.

## Contractual Safeguards

### Minimum Deployment Clause (Anti-Squatting)
* Partner must deploy FDMS to a minimum of **10 active fiscalizing clients within 24 months**.
* If not met: exclusivity converts to non-exclusive. Partner keeps perpetual IP use, but we can sell to competitors in that ecosystem.

### Territory
* Exclusivity covers **Zimbabwe only**.
* SADC expansion (Zambia, Malawi, Mozambique, Botswana) available at **+40%** of original buyout per country.

### IP Scope
* Partner receives full source code. May modify, rebrand, redistribute to their ERP clients.
* Partner may **not** sublicense the source code itself to a competing ERP consultancy.

### Support Terms
* All post-sale support is hourly, no minimum commitment.
* Critical/emergency fixes available within **4 business hours** at emergency rate.
* ZIMRA API updates offered proactively — partner decides whether to engage us or handle internally (they own the code).
