Fiscal Device Registration Required

Cannot Perform Fiscal Day Operation

A fiscal day cannot be opened or closed without an active registered fiscal device in the system.

ZIMRA Compliance Requirement:
Fiscal day operations require an authenticated fiscal device bound to a valid Device ID, Activation Key, and secp256r1 mTLS Certificate to calculate signed Z-Reports and maintain legal receipt counters.

How to Resolve:
  1. Click Register Device Now below.
  2. Enter your numeric Device ID and ZIMRA Activation Key.
  3. Click Register Device & Issue Certificate to generate keys and activate.
